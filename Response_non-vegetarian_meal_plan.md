**Meal Preparation Tips**

1. Use a variety of spices to add flavor to your meals.
2. Cook with minimal oil to reduce calorie intake.
3. Use coconut chutney as a condiment to add flavor and nutrition.
4. Include a source of protein in each meal, such as eggs, chicken, or fish.
5. Use brown rice instead of white rice to increase fiber intake.
6. Cook vegetables with minimal water to retain their nutrients.
