**Meal Preparation Tips**

1. Use a variety of spices to add flavor to your meals.
2. Cook idlis and dosas in advance and store them in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
3. Use a pressure cooker to cook dal and other lentils quickly.
4. Roast chana and peanuts in advance and store them in an airtight container.
5. Use a non-stick pan to cook chapatis and other flatbreads.
6. Store leftover food in the refrigerator and reheat it as needed.
